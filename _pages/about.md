<p class="section-kicker">01 / ACADEMIC INTRODUCTION</p>
## Research across finite many-electron systems
</div>
<p class="section-intro">IIT Mandi · School of Physical Sciences · Ph.D. scholar since 2022</p>
</div>
<p>My research interests are in theoretical and computational atomic, molecular, and optical physics. I examine photoionization, electron correlation, and collective electronic response in finite systems, with attention to photoelectron angular distributions, interatomic Coulombic decay, and attosecond time delays.</p>
<p>My current research directions include angular-distribution studies of atomic clusters, plasmon-assisted ICD in endohedral systems, and time-delay investigations. The project pages describe their present status without presenting work in progress as published results.</p>
</section>
