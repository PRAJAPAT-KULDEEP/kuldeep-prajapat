# Correlated electron dynamics in atomic and molecular systems

I study how electron correlation and collective electronic excitations shape photoionization and ultrafast dynamics in atomic clusters, fullerenes, and endohedral systems.

My work brings together theoretical models and computational approaches to explore electronic processes across femtosecond and attosecond timescales.

## Research themes

<div class="cards" markdown="1">
<div markdown="1">
<p class="meta">01 / CLUSTERS &amp; FULLERENES</p>
### Collective electronic excitations

How delocalized electrons in atomic clusters and fullerene cages respond to light and influence ionization.
</div>
<div markdown="1">
<p class="meta">02 / PHOTOIONIZATION</p>
### Correlated electron dynamics

How electron–electron interactions shape photoelectron energies, emission angles, and the structure left behind after ionization.
</div>
<div markdown="1">
<p class="meta">03 / ULTRAFAST PROCESSES</p>
### Interatomic decay and time delays

Interatomic Coulombic decay, plasmonic resonant ICD, and temporal features of photoionization from femtoseconds to attoseconds.
</div>
</div>
</section>

<section markdown="1">
## Methods and approaches

<div class="cards" markdown="1">
<div markdown="1">
### Time-dependent density functional theory

For modelling electronic response and time-dependent processes.
</div>
<div markdown="1">
### Photoelectron angular distributions

For connecting electron emission patterns with ionization dynamics and electronic structure.
</div>
<div markdown="1">
### Wigner–Smith time delays

For examining the temporal characteristics of scattering and photoionization.
</div>
<div markdown="1">
### Classical-trajectory Monte Carlo

For studying selected dynamical processes through trajectory-based simulations.
</div>
</div>
</section>
