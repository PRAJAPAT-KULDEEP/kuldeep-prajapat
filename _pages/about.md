# Correlated electron dynamics in atomic and molecular systems

<p class="hero__lede">I study how electron correlation and collective electronic excitations shape photoionization and ultrafast dynamics in atomic clusters, fullerenes, and endohedral systems.</p>

<p class="hero__support">My work brings together theoretical models and computational approaches to explore electronic processes across femtosecond and attosecond timescales.</p>
