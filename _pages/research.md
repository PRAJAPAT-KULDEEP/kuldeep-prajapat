# Research

My work and current research directions concern electronic response, photoionization, and ultrafast dynamics in atomic clusters, fullerenes, and endohedral systems. The project notes below summarize documented topics and status from my CV. They do not state unreported findings. For a non-specialist introduction to the field, read [What Is a Cluster?]({{ '/vlog/what-is-a-cluster/' | relative_url }}). Selected background references are linked in that explainer; the [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) distinguishes published work from manuscripts in preparation and conference contributions.

<div class="research-card__head"><span class="project-index">01</span><span class="status-label">MANUSCRIPT IN PREPARATION</span></div>
<h2>Photoionization and photoelectron angular distributions in sodium clusters</h2>
<p class="project-question"><strong>Research question</strong> How do sodium-cluster size and electronic structure shape energy-resolved photoionization cross sections and photoelectron angular distributions (PADs) in Na₂₀ and Na₉₂?</p>
<dl class="project-details">
<div><dt>Approach</dt><dd>Angular-distribution studies. Photoelectron angular distributions and time-dependent density functional theory are among the methods in my research toolkit.</dd></div>
<div><dt>My focus</dt><dd>Current research direction: angular-distribution studies of atomic clusters, including Na₂₀ and Na₉₂.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Status</dt><dd>Manuscript in preparation with Hari Varma R and Himadri Chakraborty. No results are presented here as published findings.</dd></div>
</dl>

<h2>Photoionization of fullerenes: energy- and angle-resolved distributions in C₆₀ and C₂₄₀</h2>
<p class="project-question"><strong>Research question</strong> How do fullerene electronic structure and collective plasmon excitations shape photoionization cross sections and energy- and angle-resolved photoelectron distributions in C₆₀ and C₂₄₀?</p>

<article class="research-card" id="plasmon-assisted-icd">
<div class="research-card__head"><span class="project-index">03</span><span class="status-label">ONGOING RESEARCH</span></div>
<h2>Plasmonic interatomic and intercluster Coulombic decay (ICD/RICD)</h2>
<p class="project-question"><strong>Research question</strong> How do collective plasmon excitations, electronic hybridization, and resonant energy transfer shape intercluster Coulombic decay (ICD), resonant ICD (RICD), and the emitted-electron spectrum in Na₂₀@C₂₄₀?</p>
<dl class="project-details">
<div><dt>Approach</dt><dd>Subshell-resolved analysis of plasmonic resonant intercluster Coulombic decay, channel interference, and photoelectron angular distributions.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Fano analysis</dt><dd>A current direction is the Fano line-shape analysis of a single-electron ICD resonance. The resonance position, width, asymmetry parameter, and resonant fraction help characterize interference between resonant and non-resonant pathways.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Status</dt><dd>Related Na₂₀@C₂₄₀ work includes the published study <em>Plasmonic Resonant Intercluster Coulombic Decay</em> and a manuscript in preparation on Fano analysis. See the <a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">publications page</a> for status details.</dd></div>
</dl>
</article>

<article class="research-card" id="photoemission-time-delays">
<div class="research-card__head"><span class="project-index">04</span><span class="status-label">RESEARCH DIRECTION</span></div>
<h2>Attosecond photoemission: Wigner–Smith time delays and streaking</h2>
<p class="project-question"><strong>Research question</strong> What do Wigner–Smith (EWS) photoionization time delays and streaking time delays reveal about ultrafast electron emission from clusters, fullerenes, and endohedral systems?</p>
<dl class="project-details">
<div><dt>Approach</dt><dd>Wigner–Smith photoionization time delays, as listed among my research methods.</dd></div>
<div><dt>My focus</dt><dd>Attosecond photoemission time delays in metal clusters, fullerenes, and endohedral systems.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Status</dt><dd>A presentation on Wigner time delay in the spillover plasmon resonance region of sodium clusters is listed for DAMOP 2023. Current directions are ongoing.</dd></div>
</dl>
</article>


<article class="research-card" id="endohedral-fullerene-photoionization">
<div class="research-card__head"><span class="project-index">05</span><span class="status-label">ONGOING RESEARCH</span></div>
<h2>Endohedral fullerene photoionization: Mg@C₆₀ and Ar@C₆₀</h2>
<p class="project-question"><strong>Research question</strong> How do atom–cage orbital hybridization, Coulomb-confinement resonances, collective cage plasmons, and interchannel coupling modify subshell-resolved photoionization in Mg@C₆₀ and Ar@C₆₀?</p>
<dl class="project-details">
<div><dt>Mg@C₆₀</dt><dd>Photoionization channels involving the encapsulated Mg atom and cage-derived states, including orbital hybridization and changes in angular distributions near the cage's giant plasmon resonance (GPR).</dd></div>
<div><dt>Ar@C₆₀</dt><dd>Argon 3p photoionization, Coulomb-confinement resonances, and channel-coupling effects on angular observables and photoemission time delays.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Observables and methods</dt><dd>Differential photoionization cross sections (DCSs), photoelectron angular distributions (PADs), the dipole anisotropy parameter β, density-functional theory (DFT), and linear-response time-dependent density-functional theory (LR-TDDFT).</dd></div>
</dl>
</article>
