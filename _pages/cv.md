## Research profile

I study the structural and ultrafast electronic dynamics of atomic clusters, fullerenes, and endohedral systems. My interests include photoionization, electron correlation, collective excitations, interatomic Coulombic decay (ICD), plasmonic resonant ICD, photoelectron angular distributions, and Wigner–Smith time delays.

## Research interests

- Photoionization dynamics in plasmonic metal clusters and fullerenes
- Angular anisotropy and confinement resonances in A@C₆₀ systems
- Attosecond photoemission time delays in metal clusters, fullerenes, and endohedral systems

## Current research directions

- Angular-distribution studies of atomic clusters
- Resonant plasmonic ICD effects on photoelectron angular distributions in Mg@C₆₀ and Ar@C₆₀
- Time-delay investigations in photoionization of metal clusters and endohedral systems

## Research methods

- Time-dependent density functional theory (TDDFT)
- Photoelectron angular distributions (PADs)
- Wigner–Smith photoionization time delays
- Classical-trajectory Monte Carlo (CTMC) simulations
